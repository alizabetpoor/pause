# Persian SEO and local keyword map

Research note: 16 October 2026. These are qualitative priorities based on the services shown on the current site and a limited public search-result review. They are not search-volume or ranking estimates.

## Evidence and limits

- The homepage currently identifies six services: architectural design, technical design, interior design, 3D visualization, architectural graphics, and landscape design. It shows project names and Sari locations, but provides no case-study descriptions, pricing, phone, street address, office hours, reviews, or credentials.
- Local result pages include directories and architecture studios for Sari. A result for an architecture studio explicitly says it is based in Sari, and a local directory targets “best architecture firms in Sari.” These are evidence of existing commercial/local SERP intent, not proof of query volume or PAUSE ranking difficulty.
- Google Trends was queried with Iran as the geography, but its interactive comparison chart was inaccessible through the available research tool. No usable Trends index was collected.
- Public search results were queried with Persian local phrases. They were not a location-personalized Google Search session, so they are used only to observe result types and wording, not rankings or competition.
- Google Ads Keyword Planner was not available through an authenticated account. Google says Keyword Planner requires account setup with billing information for basic keyword ideas and supports location and language filters; no account data was available here. Search Console and Analytics configuration are also absent from this repository. Therefore volume and organic difficulty are **not available** for every term below.
- Keyword Planner’s “Competition” is advertiser competition for ads, not an organic SEO difficulty score. Do not treat those measures as interchangeable.

## Keyword-to-page strategy

| Page or section | Main query themes | Content action |
|---|---|---|
| `/` homepage | PAUSE Architecture Studio; دفتر معماری در ساری; شرکت معماری در ساری; معماری در ساری; طراحی ساختمان در ساری | Establish the studio identity and location once. Keep the primary title and description natural; do not repeat all variants. |
| Services section | طراحی معماری، طراحی فنی، طراحی داخلی، تصویرسازی سه‌بعدی، گرافیک معماری، طراحی منظر | Retain the six verified services, explain them in Persian as well as the current English copy, and avoid unverified execution, supervision, renovation, facade, or villa promises. |
| Projects section | Service terms supported by the depicted project; interior design; visualization; landscape design | Add Persian descriptions only when project details are verified. Existing project names, years, and Sari labels are not enough to invent a case study. |
| About section | استودیو معماری پاز در ساری، مازندران | Use the supplied local context and a concise accurate service summary. Add leadership only if PAUSE approves the public Persian/English name and role for the page. |
| Footer | PAUSE identity, Sari/Mazandaran, official Instagram | Keep the one verified profile and avoid unlinked or unverified social accounts/contact claims. |

Do not create separate service pages yet. Current service descriptions are short, and project cards do not contain enough unique process, scope, outcome, or project facts for useful detail pages. Once the studio supplies those materials, distinct pages for confirmed services such as architectural design, interior design, or 3D visualization can be evaluated. Do not make location-swapped duplicate pages.

## Priority order

1. **Local firm intent:** دفتر معماری در ساری / شرکت معماری در ساری / معماری در ساری. These match the verified location and are specific to the desired lead audience.
2. **Verified core work:** طراحی ساختمان در ساری / طراحی معماری / طراحی داخلی در ساری. These map directly to current service content.
3. **Specific confirmed services:** تصویرسازی معماری / رندر معماری, طراحی منظر, گرافیک معماری. Prioritize with project proof and clear Persian service explanations.
4. **Conditional plan terminology:** نقشه‌کشی and طراحی پلان are relevant to construction drawings, but verify exact deliverable scope with the studio before promising a plan service.
5. **Keep broad or unverified terms secondary:** generic architecture/design queries are broad; villa, facade, renovation, execution, and supervision terms require explicit service confirmation.
